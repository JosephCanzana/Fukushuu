What "fukushuu" means

復習 (fukushuu) is the Japanese word for "review." It's the study habit the app is named after — going back over material on purpose, at the right moment, instead of cramming it once and hoping it sticks.

Fukushuu is a flashcard app built around that habit. Cards live in decks, decks can carry tags, and every card tracks its own review history: how easy it's been, how long since the last pass, and exactly when it's due again.

why it helps

Forgetting has a schedule. So does Fukushuu.

Timed to your memory

The SM-2 algorithm pushes a card's next review further out every time you get it right, and pulls it back in the moment you don't.

A pace per card

Every card keeps its own easiness factor, so a stubborn verb conjugation gets reviewed far more often than the fact you've never once missed.

Short, honest sessions

Only cards that are actually due show up, so a review session stays short enough to finish before the coffee gets cold.
